SPIKEY × SCREEN SETUP

Spikey lives on your laptop screen, and your other monitors stay free for work. Detect your screens, check that the laptop is marked MAIN, and copy the command. Come back here when you add the TV and want a second screen.

01 · DETECT SCREENS

Click a screen once to make it MAIN, click it again to make it SIDE, and a third time to clear it.
If detection isn't allowed: drag this window onto a screen, then press one of these buttons:

MAIN not set
SIDE not set

03 · INSTALL

  1. Press COPY COMMAND.
  2. Open PowerShell from the Start menu, paste with right-click, and press Enter.
  3. You should see "Spikey dock launch installed". Then unplug and replug the dock.
  4. The microphone is pre-approved for Spikey's own Chrome window, so you won't be asked every time.
